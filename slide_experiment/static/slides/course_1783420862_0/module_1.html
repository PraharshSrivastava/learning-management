What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

  • AI: Simulating Human Cognition
  • AI uses machines to mimic human processes like learning, reasoning, and decision-making. It leverages data and algorithms for enhanced accuracy and autonomy. [IMAGE: img_13]

Core AI Pillars

Machine Learning (ML)

Systems learn patterns directly from historical data without explicit coding.

Deep Learning

Utilizes multi-layered neural networks to simulate complex human brain functions.

Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Enables machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language.

AI Capability Spectrum

Narrow AI (ANI)

Task-Specific Design

ANI performs defined tasks (e.g., chess, driving). This is the only AI currently operational.

General AI (AGI)

Human-Level Cognition

AGI is theoretical; it represents machine intelligence matching human capability.

AI in Action: Capabilities and Applications

  • Generative AI: Creates novel content (text, images, audio, video) using LLMs.
  • AI Powers Experiences: Drives digital assistants (Alexa, Siri, Gemini) and advanced search ranking. [IMAGE: img_15]
  • Content Generation: Tools automate article drafting, email composition, and digital art creation.
