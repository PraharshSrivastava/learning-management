The Threat of Synthetic Media

AI Mimicry

Deepfakes are AI-generated media that mimic real people's appearance and speech.

Risk Vectors

These media pose risks for scams, political manipulation, and reputation damage.

Intellectual Property and Copyright Risks

Copyright Infringement Risk

AI models train on vast internet content, raising copyright and IP concerns. Artists and authors are currently suing AI companies for using their work without permission.

Workforce Transformation by AI Automation

Task Replacement

AI automation is reshaping the workforce by replacing repetitive tasks.

Job Vulnerability

Jobs like data entry, customer service, and basic copywriting are at risk.

Upskilling Imperative

Workers must upskill to remain competitive, even as AI creates new tech jobs.
