AI Fundamentals: Data as Fuel

Data as Fuel

AI requires data to function, analogous to how a car needs fuel.

Structured Data

This is organized information, exemplified by Excel spreadsheets containing rows of numbers, dates, and names.

Unstructured Data

This includes everyday information such as photos, emails, and voice recordings. [IMAGE: img_24]

Data Integrity vs. Algorithmic Autonomy: Two Paths to Intelligence

Rule-Based Systems: The Explicit Instruction Model

  • Relies on humans writing strict rules for specific tasks.
  • Adheres to the 'Garbage in, garbage out' principle, highlighting the critical importance of data quality for accurate results.

Machine Learning: The Pattern Recognition Paradigm

  • Enables computers to determine rules autonomously through data analysis.
  • Operates like teaching a child to recognize a dog using 50 examples, where the system identifies underlying patterns.

AI Training Pillars

Supervised Learning

AI learns from labeled data where the correct answers are already provided.

Unsupervised Learning

AI identifies hidden patterns within unlabeled data to find natural groupings.

Reinforcement Learning

This pillar is mentioned as a primary method but lacks specific descriptive content in the source material.

Reinforcement Learning and Deep Learning

  • Reinforcement learning teaches AI optimal strategies using a system of rewards and penalties.
  • Example of reinforcement learning: Teaching an AI to play a video game through trial and error.
  • After millions of trials, the AI learns the best strategy to win the game.
  • Deep Learning is recognized as an advanced form of Machine Learning.
  • Artificial Neural Networks, the basis of Deep Learning, are inspired by the structure of the human brain.
