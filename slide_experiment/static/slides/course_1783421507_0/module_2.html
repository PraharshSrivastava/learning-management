Daily Check-In Procedure

Step 1

Log in to the PhillipX CRM mobile application. [IMAGE: img_27]

Step 2

Tap the 'Check In' button on the home screen.

Step 3

Review the confirmation message displayed after initiating the check-in.

Finalizing Check-In and Location Confirmation

  • Click OK to formally confirm your check-in.
  • Grant GPS location access when prompted by the system. [IMAGE: img_30]
  • System registers check-in status upon successful location verification.

Sales User Commitments

Daily Check-In

Mandatory for Sales Users to maintain system access and status.

Lead Assignment Dependency

Completion of the daily check-in is required before lead assignments are distributed.

Availability Update

This process automatically sets the Sales User's operational status for the current day.

Pre-Check-In Requirements

  • Enable Location Services on your device.
  • Confirm you have a stable internet connection before starting.
