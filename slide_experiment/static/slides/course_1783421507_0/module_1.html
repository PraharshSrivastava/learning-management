PhillipX CRM Mobile App Setup

Step 1

Download the PhillipX CRM mobile app from Google Play (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS).

Step 2

Launch the app and enter your registered email or username. [IMAGE: img_22]

Step 3

Input the password provided by the system administrator during the first login.

Security & Daily Usage Mandates

Biometric Access

Enable fingerprint or facial recognition upon prompt for faster, more secure logins.

Daily Login Requirement

Sales Users must log into the mobile app every day to receive lead assignments and updates.

System Status & Manager Alerts

  • Manager Notification on Login
  • System automatically alerts your reporting manager upon successful login, confirming your active status for the day.
