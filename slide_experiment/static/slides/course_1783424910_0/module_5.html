WARNING: Inherited Bias in AI Systems

Risk of Algorithmic Bias

AI systems inherit human flaws, mistakes, and prejudices from their creators. Algorithmic bias occurs when AI is trained on biased data, following the 'Garbage in, garbage out' principle. [IMAGE: img_38]

Warning: Algorithmic Bias in Practice

Bias in AI Hiring Tools

AI hiring tools can demonstrate bias by favoring certain candidates (e.g., male candidates) based on historical data.

WARNING: AI-Generated Misinformation Risk

Danger of Deepfakes

AI-generated media (deepfakes) can be weaponized for scams, political manipulation, and reputation damage.

Intellectual Property and Copyright Disputes

  • AI models frequently utilize internet content without explicit permission, leading to copyright disputes.
  • Artists and authors are actively suing AI companies over the unauthorized use of their creative work.

Workforce Transformation: Automation and Job Shifts

Task Automation

AI is automating repetitive tasks, fundamentally shifting workforce roles.

Roles At Risk

Jobs like data entry, customer service, and basic coding are facing automation risk.

Upskilling Needed

New technology-focused jobs are emerging, requiring workers to upskill.
