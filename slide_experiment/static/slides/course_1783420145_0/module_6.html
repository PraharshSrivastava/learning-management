Prompting Pitfalls: What to Avoid

Don't treat AI like a search engine.

Vague prompts lead to vague, unhelpful outputs.

Prompting Best Practices

  • Provide context and clear instructions to achieve better results from AI.
  • Utilize the Role + Context + Task + Format formula for structuring prompts. [IMAGE: img_43]

Deconstructing the R+C+T+F Formula

Role

Define the AI's persona (e.g., 'fitness coach').

Context

Provide necessary background details (e.g., 'busy office worker').

Task

State the precise objective (e.g., 'create a workout plan').

Format

Specify the required output structure (e.g., 'markdown table').

Iterative AI Refinement Strategies

Prompting with Examples

Guide the AI's desired output style by providing specific examples within your prompts.

Iterative Adjustments

Refine initial results by requesting specific changes, such as shorter or more casual versions.
