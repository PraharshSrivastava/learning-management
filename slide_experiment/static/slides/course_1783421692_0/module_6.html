⚠️ AI Interaction Pitfalls

Don't use keywords like a search engine. AI needs depth, not just short prompts.

Vague input yields generic output. Always provide necessary context.

Shifting the AI Paradigm

AI as a Capable Assistant
View AI as a tool equipped with defined rules and specific operational goals.

The Four-Part Prompt Formula

Role

Define the AI's persona (e.g., 'Act as a fitness coach.').

Context

Supply necessary background details (e.g., 'I am a busy office worker.').

Task

State the precise request clearly (e.g., 'Create a 5-day workout plan.').

Format

Specify the required output structure (e.g., 'Use a markdown table.'). [IMAGE: img_43]

Refinement & Iteration Strategies

Style Guidance

Provide concrete examples to define the desired AI output tone and format.

Iterative Improvement

Continuously refine prompts and responses to drive results toward higher quality.
