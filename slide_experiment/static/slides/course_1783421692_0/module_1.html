What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

  • AI: Simulating Human Cognition
  • AI uses machines to mimic human processes like learning, reasoning, and decision-making. Systems improve via data and algorithms, not just explicit coding. [IMAGE: img_13]

Core AI Sub-Disciplines

Machine Learning

Systems learn patterns directly from historical data without explicit programming.

Deep Learning

Uses multi-layered neural networks to simulate complex human brain decision-making.

Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Enables computers to comprehend and generate human language.

AI Capability Spectrum: Current vs. Future

Option A

  • Traditional: Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)

Option B

  • Modern: ANI performs specific tasks (e.g., chess, autonomous driving) and is the only AI type currently operational.
  • Theoretical: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
  • Theoretical: AGI represents human-level cognition, remaining a goal of ongoing research.

Generative AI in Modern Applications

  • Generative AI creates original content (text, images, audio, video) using LLMs.
  • AI powers modern digital experiences: digital assistants, advanced search, and content creation tools. [IMAGE: img_15]
