⚠️ AI Bias: Inherited Flaws

AI inherits human flaws and prejudices from its data and creators.

Algorithmic bias occurs when flawed training data leads to discriminatory AI decisions. [IMAGE: img_38] (Example: Hiring tool penalized resumes mentioning 'women's').

The Deepfake Threat

  • AI-generated synthetic media mimicking real people.
  • Used for scams, political manipulation, and spreading disinformation.

Copyright Crisis in AI

Massive Data Ingestion: AI models currently scrape billions of images and articles from the internet.

Legal Backlash: Artists and journalists are actively suing AI companies over copyright violations.

Automation and Workforce Impact

  • AI automates routine tasks like data entry and basic customer service.
  • Upskilling is crucial for workers to navigate job changes and avoid displacement.
