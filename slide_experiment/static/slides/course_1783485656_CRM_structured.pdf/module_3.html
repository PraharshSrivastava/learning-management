Understanding the Lead: The Foundation of Client Acquisition

  • Definition: A lead is any individual who has the potential to become a PhillipCapital client.
  • System Requirement: Every lead must maintain a complete and detailed record within the PhillipX platform.

Step-by-Step: Creating a New Lead in PhillipX

1

Initiate Lead Creation

Navigate to 'Leads' via Quick Links and select 'Add'.

2

Mandatory Field Entry

Complete essential fields: First Name, Source, Status, Salesperson, Mobile Number, and City.

3

Optional Detail Capture

Input supplementary data: Full Name, City, State, Country, Lead Product Interest (multi-select), and Approximate Investment Amount.

4

System Automation Upon Save

Saving the lead automatically sets the status to 'Open' and schedules a follow-up meeting for the current day.

The Lead Status Lifecycle and Progression

Active Progression Path

  • Open $\rightarrow$ Interested $\rightarrow$ Converted
VS

Exit/Closure Path (Do-Not-Contact)

  • Requires mandatory selection of a Lost Status reason (e.g., Poor Fit, Pricing, Budget Constraints).
  • This status cannot be bypassed or skipped during the process.

Lead Assignment Mechanisms and Visibility Controls

Assignment Methods

Auto-assignment (based on pin code/round robin), Manual assignment by Manager, Bulk Import.

Data Visibility Rules

Access is restricted: Only the assigned Relationship Manager (RM) and their direct Manager can view the lead record.

Re-assignment Protocols

Re-assignment is strictly permitted only within the defined departmental hierarchy.

Managing Leads from Departures: Manager Oversight

  • Flagging Mechanism: Pending leads belonging to resigned salespersons are specifically flagged on the Manager Dashboard.
  • Actionable Insight: The Manager Dashboard provides a direct option to re-assign these flagged leads to active team members.
