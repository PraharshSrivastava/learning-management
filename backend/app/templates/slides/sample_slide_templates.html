Comparison
Before / after

Compare behaviors, not just columns.

Weak slide pattern

  • Every idea is placed inside a similar card.
  • Titles describe topics but not the message.
  • Color changes are decorative instead of meaningful.

Premium pattern

  • The layout matches the relationship in the content.
  • The headline says what the learner should remember.
  • Accent color is reserved for meaning and emphasis.
